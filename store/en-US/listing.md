## Full description (max 4000)
```
Recordish is a simple notes app with a twist: every note is either private or public — you choose with one tap.

■ Private notes
Private is the default. Only you can see these notes.
Jot down ideas, to-dos, plans, or anything you want to keep to yourself.
Your private list stays hidden until you tap to show it, so nobody can peek over your shoulder.

■ Public notes — only 2 a day
Share a thought with everyone, but only up to 2 public notes per day.
No likes, no follower counts, no endless feed to keep up with. Just a calm stream of short notes from real people.

■ Why you'll like it
· Sign in with your Google account in seconds
· The same notes on your phone and on the web (recordishapp.com)
· Long notes are folded, and open with a tap
· Copy any note with one tap
· Your Google name is never shown. You get an automatic nature-style pen name (like "Running Wolf") and can change it anytime
· Report inappropriate public notes and block users you don't want to see
· Delete your account and all your data at any time, right from the app

No ads. Just notes.

Note: the app's menus and buttons are currently in Korean.
```
